03

Three pillars of decentralized design

Launchpad

Permissionless Creation

Anyone can create a market. Creators deposit capital, become the first LP, and earn ongoing yield from trading fees. Economic alignment without gatekeepers.

ZK Rules

Trustless Settlement

Adjudicators report the outcome and when it became true (T*). Trades after T* are rewound via ZK proofs. Four adjudicator classes for every trust model.

TickBook

On-Chain CLOB

10,000-tick orderbook with bitmap O(1) discovery. CEX-grade execution, fully on-chain. No off-chain sequencer, no MEV extraction.

The Gaming Trader's Edge

Alice bets $200 on YES, paying $10 in fees. She receives $10 in LP tokens. The market goes viral with $50K volume. Her LP tokens now represent ~$2,500 in fees. Even if her prediction loses, her LP position offsets the loss.

07

T* retroactive finality

When news breaks, fast bots exploit stale AMM prices. Sooth's T* mechanism rewinds post-truth trades, protecting LPs.

14:00:00 — BTC crosses $100K T* = this moment 14:00:05 — News breaks 14:00:10 — Bot buys YES at 60¢ exploit attempt 14:00:30 — Adjudicator reports: YES, T* = 14:00:00 Settlement: Bot's trade REWOUND. LPs protected.
