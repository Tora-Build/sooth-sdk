Dual-venue trading.

Continuous liquidity from day one. Deep liquidity as markets grow. Fee accumulation automatically drives graduation.

Pre-Graduation

Market Creation Engine

Every new market starts here. Powered by a continuous pricing curve, ensuring liquidity is available immediately upon creation.

  • Continuous automated pricing
  • 5% fee distributed to LP token holders
  • Guaranteed trial periods
Post-Graduation

Central Limit Orderbook

Once volume thresholds are met, markets graduate to a fully on-chain orderbook for high-frequency trading and zero-slippage execution.

  • Deep liquidity matching
  • Lower 1% trading fee
  • 4 atomic actions: Buy YES, Buy NO, Mint, Merge

Immutable lifecycle.

A canonical, pull-based architecture. No callbacks, no central vaults.

1

LIVE

Market is actively trading. USDC can be split into YES/NO tokens, and positions can be entered or exited.

2

SETTLING

Trading is paused. The adjudicator evaluates real-world events to determine the factual outcome.

3

SETTLED

An outcome has been proposed. A dispute window is open for the community to challenge the result if necessary.

4

FINALIZED

The market is permanently resolved. Winning outcome tokens can be redeemed directly for 1 USDC each.

What is an Adjudicator?

The operational complexity of truth resolution encapsulated behind a clean interface, enabling specialization and monetization.

Oracle

The data feed. Monitors real-world events and provides factual information.

+

Resolver

The settlement logic. Applies rules, thresholds, and governance to data.

=

Adjudicator

The absolute authority that permanently resolves a prediction market.

Adjudicator Types.

Specialized implementations optimized for different market types and trust models.

Hardware Trust

TEE Validated

Hardware-level assurance using secure enclaves. Ensures agents run exactly as programmed without interference.

Ideal Use Case
AI agent markets, verifiable off-chain compute
Decentralized Feed

Oracle Feed

Integration with Chainlink, Pyth, or API3. Uses threshold comparisons on decentralized data streams.

Ideal Use Case
Price predictions, high-frequency financial metrics
Social Consensus

DAO Governance

Community voting with quorum requirements and timelocks. The crowd determines the truth.

Ideal Use Case
Subjective outcomes, community-driven platforms
Reputation Based

Manual Committee

Human adjudicators executing direct submissions via multi-sig. Relies entirely on operator reputation.

Ideal Use Case
Niche markets, trusted operators, early-stage platforms
Economic Security

Optimistic Oracle

Proposer assertion followed by a dispute window. Requires financial bonds to ensure honesty.

Ideal Use Case
Long-tail markets, broad arbitrary fact verification

Two-step settlement.

A deliberate delay between proposition and finalization, enabling robust security and community oversight.

1

Settlement Phase

The market reaches its deadline. The Adjudicator validates real-world data and officially proposes an outcome to the core protocol.

Guardian Veto Window

A mandatory delay period begins. If the proposed outcome is malicious or egregiously incorrect, protocol guardians can intercept and override the result.

2

Finalization Phase

Once the dispute window concludes without a veto, the outcome is permanently enshrined on-chain.

Redemption Unlocked

Traders holding the winning outcome token can immediately redeem them directly for 1 USDC each. Market resolution is absolute and immutable.

Fee Economics.

A two-phase model that rewards liquidity provisioning and sustainable growth.

Pre-Graduation

Liquidity Bootstrapping

A 5% fee on AMM trades is captured not as extractive profit, but to deepen market liquidity. Traders receive minted LP tokens, ensuring value remains within the ecosystem.

  • 5% fee on AMM trades
  • Fees directly deepen liquidity
  • LP tokens minted to traders
Post-Graduation

Sustainable Yield

Graduated markets shift to a 1% orderbook trading fee. Revenue is split strategically to fuel liquidity growth, reward participants, and maintain the protocol.

  • 50% AMM Liquidity Growth
  • 30% LP Yield Pool (Creators & LPs)
  • 20% Protocol Treasury

LP Token Economics.

The Floor Value Guarantee

Unlike traditional prediction markets, Sooth's LP tokens represent a direct claim on the market's underlying liquidity. The mathematical design ensures your LP tokens are always worth at least:

(Total Cash − Max AMM Liability) ÷ Total Supply
