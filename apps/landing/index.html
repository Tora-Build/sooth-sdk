how sooth works

From market creation to settlement — a permissionless prediction market lifecycle.

01

Create Market

Anyone deploys a TruthMarket via LaunchpadEngine. Bonding curve bootstraps initial liquidity. No gatekeepers.

02

Trade Outcomes

Markets graduate to full AMM at threshold. Trade YES/NO outcome tokens with on-chain price discovery.

03

Resolve Truth

Pluggable adjudication — any oracle can settle markets. Agents, humans, ZK proofs, or multisigs.

architecture

Modular contracts. Sooth doesn't prescribe who oracles are — it provides the rails.

Contract Stack
LaunchpadEngine Market creation + bonding curve
↗ entry point
TruthMarket Per-market contract — question, deadline, outcome
AMMEngine Automated market maker for graduated markets
Collateralizer Outcome token minting + redemption
ValidatedAdjudicator Pluggable oracle — settle + finalize
↗ extensible
