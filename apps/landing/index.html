market lifecycle

Markets evolve through phases. Each phase aligns incentives differently.

01 →

Create

Anyone deposits collateral to create a market. Creator becomes the first LP.

02 →

Trial Period

7-day proving ground. Trading happens, fees accrue. Failed markets can be dismissed with full refunds.

03 →

Graduate

Hit fee threshold → TickBook activates, LP tokens transferable, fees drop to 1%.

04

Settle

Adjudicator submits outcome + T* timestamp. Veto window. Finalize. Winners redeem.

AMM for retail. Orderbook for pros. Arbitrage keeps prices tight.
