Core Mechanics

01

Dual-Venue Trading

LMSR AMM guarantees retail liquidity, upgrading seamlessly to a 10,000-tick orderbook (SoothBook) upon graduation.

  • [+] Constant-time tick discovery
  • [+] Zero-slippage limit orders
  • [+] Bounded LP exposure
02

Retroactive Finality

Settlement uses truth-timestamping (T*). Trades executed post-event but prior to settlement are retroactively nullified.

  • [+] Neutralizes fast-finger exploitation
  • [+] Removes forced liquidation timers
  • [+] Objective historical truth
03

Creator Economics

Market creators bootstrap liquidity, capturing permanent LP shares. Protocol fees stream directly to creators dynamically.

  • [+] 7-day trial period protection
  • [+] 5% bonding fee → LP yield
  • [+] 1% graduated trading fee
