# Sooth Protocol Media Kit

Official brand assets for Sooth Protocol.

## The Layered Truth
The primary mark of Sooth Protocol. It represents the modular architecture of the protocol: Market Layer (Top), Liquidity Layer (Middle), and Settlement Layer (Bottom).

### Primary Assets
- `sooth-logo.svg` - Official Dapp Logo (Layered, Dark BG)
- `sooth-social-banner.svg` - Social Media Banner (1500x500)

### Mediakit Variants
- `mediakit/sooth-logo-layered-light.svg` - High Contrast (Light BG)
- `mediakit/sooth-logo-layered-mono.svg` - Monochrome (Black & White)
- `mediakit/sooth-logo-layered-no-bg.svg` - Transparent Background
- `mediakit/sooth-social-bg-no-text.svg` - Pure Social Background (No Typography)

## Brand Identity
Sooth is built on the concept of **Retroactive Finality**. Our visual identity emphasizes depth, layers, and the vertical alignment of truth from data to settlement.

## Colors
- **Truth (Market):** `#22D3EE` (Cyan)
- **Yield (Liquidity):** `#C084FC` (Purple)
- **Finality (Settlement):** `#818CF8` (Indigo)
- **Core (Void):** `#020617` (Deep Slate)
