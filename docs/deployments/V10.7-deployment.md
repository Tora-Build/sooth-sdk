## What Changed in V10.7

### The Bug
In V10.0-V10.6, spot sells at **better prices** were missed during crossing:
- User places NO spot sell at ticks 100-110 (asking 10-11%)
- Buyer places NO order at ticks 1-500 (willing to pay up to 50%)
- **Expected**: Order crosses the spot sell ✅
- **V10.6 Reality**: Order doesn't cross ❌

### Root Cause
`_unifiedCross()` passed a narrow "overlap range" to `_fillSpotLiquidity()`. This overlap was calculated based on range-vs-range order crossings and only covered the market-clearing price zone (e.g., ticks 430-500). Spot sells at lower ticks (100-110) were completely invisible to the search.

### The Fix
Modified `TickBookPoolManager._unifiedCross()` (lines 1111-1131) to:
1. Extract buyer's full tick range from the new order
2. Convert to seller's native tick space (NO buyer's range → YES-equivalent for searching)
3. Pass this full range to `_fillSpotLiquidity()`

Now the search covers the buyer's **entire price tolerance**, finding ALL spot sells that should cross.

### Gas Impact
- **Before**: ~350k gas
- **After**: ~361k gas
- **Increase**: +11k gas (3.1%)
- **Reason**: Wider tick search range
- **Verdict**: Acceptable tradeoff for correctness
