  <project_name>Sooth Protocol</project_name>

  <overview>
    Sooth is a fully on-chain protocol for flexible-end prediction markets that enables retroactive finality through a modular V9 architecture. Unlike traditional markets that force expiry, Sooth allows markets to settle retroactively based on truth time (T*), neutralizing latency arbitrage and protecting liquidity providers. The protocol features a Dual DEX Engine with two complementary trading venues: a continuous LMSR-based AMM for passive liquidity and a sophisticated orderbook (TickBook) for active market makers. V9 introduces a singleton architecture with flash accounting, Uniswap V4-style hooks, and improved developer ergonomics. The system consists of modular, composable contracts with clear domain separation including TruthMarket (lifecycle authority), LaunchpadEngine (creator economics), AMMEngine (LMSR trading), TickBookPoolManager (orderbook venue), Collateralizer (token split/merge), and hook infrastructure for extensibility.
  </overview>
